About

Environmental research · Geospatial analysis · Climate science

I am an environmental and geospatial researcher interested in understanding environmental and climatological processes through geospatial technologies, remote sensing and data-driven modelling.

My academic and research interests include climate science, hydrometeorology, environmental modelling, geospatial artificial intelligence and geospatial data science.

I am particularly interested in using geospatial data and computational approaches to investigate environmental patterns and support evidence-based research.
