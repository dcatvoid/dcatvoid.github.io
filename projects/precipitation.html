RESEARCH PROJECT

Wavelet Decomposed Short Term Forecasting of Daily Precipitation Using Artificial Neural Networks in Khulna, Bangladesh

Climate Modelling · Time Series Analysis · Wavelet Transformation · ANN

Overview

This research focuses on short-term daily precipitation forecasting for Khulna, Bangladesh. The workflow combines time-series analysis, signal decomposition and artificial neural network modelling.

Research Focus

Precipitation series are analysed using wavelet-based decomposition and neural-network approaches. Model performance is assessed using statistical and hydrological evaluation measures, including R², RMSE, MAE, CE and KGE.
