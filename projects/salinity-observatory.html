Overview

This study investigates soil salinity in the coastal districts of Khulna, Satkhira, and Jessore through field-based soil sampling and laboratory electrical conductivity (EC) measurements. The study involved collecting 2,170 topsoil samples during March 2026, following the standard soil-water (1:5) extraction procedure.

Laboratory-derived EC measurements were used to characterize spatial variations in soil salinity and develop bubble-density maps. The research provides georeferenced field observations for understanding coastal soil salinity patterns and supports the integration of ground measurements with remote sensing data for future salinity monitoring and modelling.
