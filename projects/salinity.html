RESEARCH PROJECT

Soil Salinity Prediction

Soil Salinity · Remote Sensing · Machine Learning · SHAP

Overview

This project explores the prediction of soil salinity from environmental and remote-sensing information using machine-learning methods.

Research Focus

Candidate models used in the modelling workflow include Artificial Neural Networks, Random Forest, XGBoost and LightGBM. SHAP is used for interpreting model contributions.

Project Materials

Study-area maps, feature-importance plots, validation results and salinity prediction maps can be added here.
