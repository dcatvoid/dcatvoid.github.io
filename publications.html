Integrating Machine Learning, Remote Sensing, and In-Situ Data to Map Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) in Sundarbans Mangrove Forest

This study integrates 173 in-situ soil samples with soil properties, Sentinel-2 spectral indices, terrain attributes and micro-climatic variables to estimate the spatial distribution of soil organic carbon in the Sundarbans mangrove forest. Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) and Genetic Algorithm (GA) feature selection are combined with six machine-learning models.
